Description:
A glitter unicorn flaps across your screen while candy fireworks burst behind it. Peppermints, wrapped candies, jellybeans, stars, and hearts rain down, then the page goes back to normal.

You set the rules:
- How often: random, or every 1 to 60 minutes
- Unicorn size and flight speed
- Sparkle trail on or off
- 1 to 50 candy fireworks per flight
- Skip any site with one click
- Fly now button for an instant show
- Random flights are opt-in: turn them on in settings when you want them

Built to stay out of your way:
- The unicorn never blocks clicks. Links and buttons keep working mid-flight.
- Flies only in the tab you're looking at.
- Scheduled flights pause when your computer has reduce motion turned on.
- No accounts, no tracking, no data collected. Your settings stay in your Chrome profile.

Single purpose description:
Candy Unicorn shows a decorative animated unicorn with candy fireworks across the web page you're viewing, at times the user chooses.

Permission justifications:

activeTab:
When the user clicks the toolbar icon and presses Fly now, the extension shows the unicorn animation on the current tab only.

storage:
Saves the user's settings, such as how often the unicorn appears, its size and speed, firework count, and skipped sites.

alarms:
Schedules the next unicorn flight at the random or fixed interval the user picks.

scripting:
Adds the unicorn animation layer to the current tab when the user presses Fly now, or when a scheduled flight starts after the user has turned on random flights.

Optional host permission (<all_urls>), requested at runtime only:
Not granted at install. Users who want the unicorn to fly in on its own press "Allow random flights" in the settings, and Chrome asks them to approve. The extension then draws a temporary animation layer on the page they are viewing. It does not read, collect, change, or send page content. Users turn this off at any time from the same settings panel.
